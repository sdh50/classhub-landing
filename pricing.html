Questions teachers actually ask

Do I need my district's approval to use this?

No district contract or IT approval process is required to sign up — this works the same way tools like Wayground or DeltaMath do, as something you choose to use in your own classroom. That said, it's always worth knowing your own district's policy on third-party tools that touch student data.

Is my students' data safe?

Your class site is privately hosted and only accessible to people you've given a join code, class invite, or family code to. Security has been a priority from day one — especially given how this project started.

Will I actually be charged after 30 days?

Only if you don't cancel first. Your card is on file so nothing interrupts mid-semester, but you can cancel any time from your account before the trial ends and you won't be charged at all.

Can my school pay instead of me?

Right now billing is set up per individual teacher. If that's a blocker for your situation, reach out directly and we can figure out the right option.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

It stays exactly where it is — nothing is deleted just for canceling. Reach out directly any time if you want your data exported or fully removed.

Can I bring my existing Canvas course over?

Yes — export your course as a Canvas Common Cartridge (.imscc) file and it imports directly, including modules, assignments, and quiz questions with embedded images.
